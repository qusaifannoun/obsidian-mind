## Cross-ticket tensions
- **🔴 A — aircraft-type-on-TOR ownership (410 ↔ 422):** 410 AC-02 (TOR "linked to type-training qualifications" at creation) vs 422 (instructor "adds an aircraft certificate"). **Nothing writes `tor.aircraftTypeIds`** → keystone gap. `assertAircraftOnTor` in 422's create self-blocks (requires what it's meant to create). Needs single-owner resolution + derive/populate `aircraftTypeIds` from approved quals.
- **🟠 B — "requested role/qualification" orphaned (410 AC-05):** Draft→Active needs a "requested role" concept, unowned → Form 32 shows all 4 (A/B/C/D) regardless of role (violates 415 AC-02).
- **🟠 C — 411 is a dependency hub:** Active/Paused logic must track every requirement type's valid/approved definition (412/413/414/415-6/417-421/422); requirement-ticket changes silently affect 411.
- **🟡 D — sit-in handoff (429 → 421):** sit-in created in 429 (tat-ws enrollment) but lives in 421 (History Form).
- **🟡 E — assessment ↔ qualification (423 → 422):** both per-aircraft-type; assessment references `qualificationId`.
- **⚪ F — 436 outlier:** tat-ws online-course concern, loosely in-epic.
