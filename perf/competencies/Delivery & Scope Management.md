# Delivery & Scope Management

## Definition

Turning a ticket into the *correct* deliverable: catching when scope is wrong (mis-filed repo, "greenfield" work that already exists), splitting and sequencing the work, shipping incrementally, and raising contradictions/dependencies to the right people rather than building blindly.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Working | Delivers the ticket as written |
| Strong | Questions and corrects scope; ships in safe increments |
| Lead | Reframes ambiguous work, manages cross-team dependencies, prevents wasted effort |

## Growth Notes

Keep flagging contradictions early (ticket vs. code vs. backend) and choosing approaches that auto-adapt to later decisions (e.g. permission-based gating).
